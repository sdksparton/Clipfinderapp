Why it's different

Regular transcript search only finds exact words. ClipFinder understands meaning.

Ctrl+F on a transcript

money budget cost pay

Only finds the word "money." If the professor said "budget," you get nothing.

ClipFinder

money budget cost pay

Word vectors (Word2Vec-style) know these words mean similar things, so "money" also finds "budget," "cost," and "pay."
